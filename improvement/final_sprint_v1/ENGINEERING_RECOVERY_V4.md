## Spatial transport

partly outside the image was repeatedly rejected. The repaired tracker preserves
the transported full polygon, computes its exact positive-area image
intersection, and uses only that visible region for the next feature mask and
crop. Empty or degenerate intersections still require fresh grounding. Existing
flow, cut, scale, inlier, periodic and per-source call limits remain unchanged.
The semantic-extent control uses the same visible geometry. This is a new
spatial implementation and needs its own real smoke and throughput forecast.
